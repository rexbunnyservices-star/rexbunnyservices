## Long Description (for About/Profile pages)

REX Bunny Services is a marketing agency specializing in making brands visible across every search channel in 2026. As search has fractured into traditional engines (Google, Bing), AI chatbots (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity), voice assistants (Siri, Alexa), and AI Overviews, most agencies only cover one channel. We cover all three.

**SEO (Search Engine Optimization):** Technical SEO, local SEO, and programmatic multi-location optimization at scale. Automated city-page generation, entity disambiguation, GSC integration, and rank tracking.

**GEO (Generative Engine Optimization):** Getting your brand cited as a source inside AI responses. Entity engineering, LLM citation optimization, AI crawl strategy (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot), source score boosting, and multi-engine tracking.

**AEO (Answer Engine Optimization):** Making your content the direct answer in featured snippets, Google AI Overviews, voice search, Siri, and Alexa. Direct answer engineering, FAQ/HowTo schema, voice search readiness, and snippet capture.

**Web Development:** High-performance websites built with Astro, Preact Islands, and Tailwind CSS. Lighthouse 95+ guaranteed, sub-second load times, edge-deployed, and LLM-optimized HTML. Optional add-on to any marketing package.
