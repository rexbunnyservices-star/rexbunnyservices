## The Scale Problem

Multi-location businesses face a paradox: they need city-specific landing pages to rank locally, but creating 500+ unique pages manually is impractical. The solution is programmatic SEO — automated page generation with structured data, unique content, and proper internal linking.

## How It Works

### 1. Data-Driven Templates
We build page templates that pull from structured data sources: location names, services offered, local landmarks, and regional attributes. Each page is unique because the data feeding it is unique.

### 2. Automated Schema Markup
Every location page gets `LocalBusiness` schema with:
- `name`, `address`, `telephone`
- `geo` coordinates
- `openingHours`
- `areaServed`
- `sameAs` links to Google Business Profile

### 3. Content Variation
No two pages are identical. Our templates vary:
- Service descriptions by region
- Testimonials from local customers
- Case studies relevant to the area
- Local landmark references

### 4. Monitoring at Scale
We use n8n workflows to monitor page health: HTTP status, Core Web Vitals, indexation status, and AI crawl frequency. If a page drops in performance, you know immediately.

## Results

For a national service company with 300 locations:
- **82% increase** in organic traffic within 90 days
- **3.2x more** local pack appearances
- **156 city pages** indexed and ranking in top 10
