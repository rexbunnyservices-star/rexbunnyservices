## The Shift from Traditional SEO to GEO

Search is no longer just about blue links. In 2024-2026, AI-powered search engines — ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude — have fundamentally changed how users discover information. Instead of scrolling through ten results, users now ask a question and get a synthesized answer.

This is **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)**: the practice of optimizing your website so AI models cite it as a source in their responses.

## Why GEO Matters

When ChatGPT recommends a service or Perplexity answers a question, it pulls from specific websites. If your site isn't optimized for AI consumption, you're invisible to this growing segment of search traffic.

Consider this: **40% of all search queries** now happen through AI interfaces. That number is projected to reach 60% by 2028.

## The Four Pillars of GEO

### 1. Entity Optimization
AI models need to understand *who you are* and *what you do*. This means clear, structured data about your business, services, and expertise — wrapped in schema.org markup.

### 2. LLM Citation Engineering
You want AI to cite your content. That means writing authoritative, well-structured content that directly answers specific questions. Lists, statistics, and clear definitions get cited more often.

### 3. AI Crawl Optimization
If AI bots can't crawl your site, they can't cite it. You need:
- An `llms.txt` file (yes, it's a real thing)
- Proper `robots.txt` configuration allowing GPTBot, Claude-Web, and other AI crawlers
- Fast load times — AI crawlers have timeouts too

### 4. Source Score Boosting
AI models rank sources by credibility. Backlinks, domain authority, and structured content all contribute to your "source score" in AI training and retrieval.

## Getting Started with GEO

The good news? Most GEO best practices overlap with technical SEO. If your site is already fast, well-structured, and uses schema markup, you're halfway there.

The missing piece is usually the AI-specific configuration: `llms.txt`, robots.txt updates, and content structured for citation rather than just ranking.
