## What Are Google AI Overviews?

Google AI Overviews (formerly Search Generative Experience / SGE) appear at the top of Google search results for many queries. Instead of showing 10 blue links, Google generates a synthesized answer that cites specific websites. If your site is one of those cited sources, you get premium visibility — above all organic results.

As of 2026, AI Overviews appear for approximately 30% of all Google searches, up from 15% in late 2025. This number is projected to reach 50% by end of 2027.

## How Google Selects Sources

Google evaluates these signals when choosing which sites to cite:

1. **Content relevance** — Does your page directly answer the query?
2. **Entity authority** — Is your site a recognized authority on this topic?
3. **Structured data** — Do you have schema markup that helps Google understand your content?
4. **Content freshness** — Is your content up to date?
5. **E-E-A-T signals** — Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness

## 7 Tactics to Get Cited in AI Overviews

### 1. Answer the Query Directly in the First Paragraph

Google's AI extracts the most relevant paragraph. If your answer is buried in paragraph 5, it won't be extracted.

### 2. Use FAQ Schema Markup

Pages with FAQ schema are disproportionately cited in AI Overviews because Google can easily extract Q&A pairs.

### 3. Create Comparison Tables

AI Overviews love structured comparisons. Create tables that compare options, features, or pricing.

### 4. Optimize for Featured Snippets

AI Overviews often pull from featured snippet content. Target position zero with clear, concise answers.

### 5. Keep Content Fresh

Google prefers recently updated content for AI Overviews. Update your top-performing pages quarterly.

### 6. Build Topic Clusters

Create comprehensive content clusters around your core topics. Interlink related articles to demonstrate topical depth.

### 7. Add Author Credentials

Include author bios with relevant credentials. Google's AI Overviews favor content from identifiable experts.

## Measuring Your AI Overview Visibility

Track these metrics monthly:

- Number of queries where your site appears in AI Overviews
- Click-through rate from AI Overview citations
- Share of voice vs competitors in AI Overviews
