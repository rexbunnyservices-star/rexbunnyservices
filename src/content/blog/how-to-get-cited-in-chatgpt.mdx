## Why ChatGPT Cites Some Websites and Ignores Others

When someone asks ChatGPT "What's the best marketing agency for local SEO?", the model doesn't randomly pick a website. It evaluates hundreds of signals — authority, relevance, structure, trust — and selects sources that meet specific criteria. Getting cited in ChatGPT isn't luck. It's engineering.

Here's exactly how to make your brand one of those sources.

## Step 1: Build Entity Clarity

ChatGPT resolves entities before citing them. If your business name is ambiguous or your online presence is scattered, the model skips you.

**What to do:**

- **Schema.org markup on every page.** Use `Organization`, `LocalBusiness`, or `ProfessionalService` schema. Include `sameAs` links to your LinkedIn, Crunchbase, and social profiles.
- **Consistent NAP everywhere.** Name, Address, Phone must be identical across Google Business Profile, your website, directories, and social media.
- **Wikidata entry.** If you don't have one, create it. ChatGPT cross-references Wikidata for entity resolution.
- **Clear "About" page.** One page that states who you are, what you do, where you operate, and why you're credible. AI models love structured, scannable About pages.

## Step 2: Make Your Content Citation-Worthy

ChatGPT cites content that answers questions directly and authoritatively. Generic blog posts don't make the cut.

**Citation-worthy content has:**

- **Direct answers in the first paragraph.** Don't bury the lead. State the answer, then explain.
- **Specific claims with sources.** "Our clients see a 300% traffic increase" beats "we help businesses grow."
- **Structured formats.** Tables, numbered lists, and FAQ sections are easier for LLMs to extract.
- **Original data or insights.** If you can cite something unique — a case study result, proprietary methodology, original research — you're more likely to be referenced.

## Step 3: Optimize Your robots.txt

ChatGPT uses GPTBot to crawl the web. If your robots.txt blocks GPTBot, you're invisible to ChatGPT.

**Your robots.txt should include:**

```
User-agent: GPTBot
Allow: /
```

Don't block GPTBot from important pages. Allow it to crawl your services, blog, case studies, and About page. Block only admin areas and API endpoints.

## Step 4: Create an llms.txt File

`llms.txt` is a new standard (proposed by Anthropic) that gives LLMs a structured summary of your site. Place it at your domain root: `https://yoursite.com/llms.txt`

**Include:**
- Your company name and one-line description
- List of key pages with URLs
- Services summary
- FAQ section
- Contact information

This file acts as a "table of contents" for AI models. When ChatGPT crawls your site, llms.txt gives it a quick overview of what you offer and where to find detailed information.

## Step 5: Build Backlink Authority

ChatGPT ranks sources by trust. Backlinks from authoritative domains signal trust.

**Priority targets:**
- Industry directories (Clutch, G2, Crunchbase)
- .edu and .gov references (if applicable)
- Wikipedia and Wikidata
- LinkedIn company page with complete profile
- Guest posts on reputable industry blogs

The key insight: ChatGPT doesn't just count backlinks — it evaluates the *quality and relevance* of linking domains. One link from a relevant .edu site outweighs fifty random directory links.

## Step 6: Test Your AI Visibility

After implementing these changes, test whether ChatGPT cites your brand:

1. Ask ChatGPT questions about your industry where your brand should appear
2. Ask Perplexity the same questions
3. Ask Google Gemini
4. Track which responses mention your brand

If you're not being cited after 2-4 weeks of optimization, revisit your entity signals and content quality.

## The Bottom Line

Getting cited in ChatGPT is a systematic process: build entity clarity, create citation-worthy content, optimize crawl access, and demonstrate authority. Most businesses haven't done any of this — which means the competition is low and the opportunity is massive.
