## The Short Answer

SEO optimizes for Google rankings. GEO optimizes for AI model citations. They overlap, but they're not the same thing.

## SEO in 2026

Traditional SEO focuses on:
- Ranking in Google's blue link results
- Earning backlinks for authority
- Optimizing for keywords and search intent
- Core Web Vitals as a ranking signal

This still matters. Google still drives billions of searches daily.

## GEO in 2026

Generative Engine Optimization focuses on:
- Being cited in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity responses
- Entity clarity so AI models understand who you are
- Structured data depth for AI consumption
- Citation-worthiness over keyword density

## Where They Overlap

Both SEO and GEO benefit from:
- Fast page loads (Core Web Vitals)
- Structured data (JSON-LD schema)
- High-quality, authoritative content
- Clean HTML structure

## Where They Diverge

| Factor | SEO | GEO |
|--------|-----|-----|
| Target | Google rankings | AI citations |
| Key signal | Backlinks | Entity clarity |
| Content style | Keyword-optimized | Question-answering |
| Technical | Meta tags, sitemap | llms.txt, schema depth |
| Measurement | Rankings, traffic | AI citation frequency |

## The Bottom Line

You need both. SEO gets you found on Google. GEO gets you recommended by AI. In 2026, the brands winning are doing both.
