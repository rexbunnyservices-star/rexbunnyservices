## Why Perplexity Matters

Perplexity AI has crossed 100 million monthly active users. Unlike ChatGPT, it's built specifically for search — and it cites sources prominently. Every citation is a direct link to your website.

## How Perplexity Finds Sources

Perplexity uses a combination of:
- Real-time web crawling
- Bing search index
- Its own crawling bot (PerplexityBot)
- Structured data from your site

## 5 Steps to Optimize for Perplexity

### 1. Allow PerplexityBot in robots.txt

```
User-agent: PerplexityBot
Allow: /
```

### 2. Create Comprehensive llms.txt

Perplexity reads `llms.txt` to understand your business. Make it detailed.

### 3. Build Citation-Worthy Content

Perplexity prefers content that:
- Answers specific questions directly
- Includes data and statistics
- Has clear, structured headings
- Cites its own sources (meta, but effective)

### 4. Implement FAQ Schema

Add `FAQPage` schema to your key pages. Perplexity uses this to match questions to answers.

### 5. Get Backlinks from Cited Sources

Perplexity tends to cite sources that other trusted sources cite. Build backlinks from industry publications.

## Measuring Perplexity Visibility

Ask Perplexity questions related to your industry and check:
- Does your brand appear in the response?
- Is your website linked as a source?
- What context is your brand mentioned in?
