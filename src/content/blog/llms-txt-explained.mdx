## What Is llms.txt?

`llms.txt` is a plain text file you place at your domain root (like `robots.txt`) that gives AI language models a structured summary of your website.

Think of it as a business card for AI. When GPTBot or Claude-Web crawls your site, `llms.txt` is one of the first things they read.

## Why It Matters

AI models need to understand:
- Who you are
- What you do
- What services you offer
- How to describe you to users

Without `llms.txt`, the AI has to infer this from your HTML. With it, you control the narrative.

## How to Create One

Create a file at `https://yoursite.com/llms.txt` with this structure:

```markdown
# Company Name
> One-line tagline

## About
Brief company description.

## Services
- [Service Name](URL): What it does

## Key Pages
- [Pricing](URL): Link to pricing
- [Case Studies](URL): Link to portfolio
```

## Best Practices

1. **Keep it concise** — 200-500 words maximum
2. **Use Markdown** — AI models parse it easily
3. **Include links** — Point to your key pages
4. **Update regularly** — Keep it current with new services
5. **Reference it in robots.txt** — Not required, but good practice
