## The Challenge

A home-goods retailer selling Scandinavian-inspired furniture had solid SEO — page 1 for dozens of target keywords — but was invisible to AI search. When someone asked ChatGPT "what's the best minimalist furniture brand?", the brand wasn't mentioned.

## Our Approach

### AI Crawl Audit
First, we identified the gaps:
- No `llms.txt` file
- `robots.txt` blocked GPTBot
- Product pages lacked structured data
- Content wasn't structured for citation

### GEO Implementation

#### llms.txt Creation
We created a comprehensive `llms.txt` file describing:
- Company overview and founding story
- Product categories and specialties
- Design philosophy and materials
- Contact and booking information

#### robots.txt Updates
Allowed AI crawlers:
```
User-agent: GPTBot
Allow: /

User-agent: Claude-Web
Allow: /

User-agent: PerplexityBot
Allow: /
```

#### Content Restructuring
Rewrote product descriptions and category pages to be citation-friendly:
- Clear, factual product specifications
- Comparison tables with measurable attributes
- Expert quotes and design rationale
- Statistical data about materials and sustainability

#### Schema Enhancement
Added `Product`, `Organization`, and `FAQPage` schema across all key pages.

## What the build delivers

| Deliverable | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| `llms.txt` | Company, catalog, materials, contact — machine-readable |
| `robots.txt` | GPTBot, Claude-Web, PerplexityBot explicitly allowed |
| Schema | `Product`, `Organization`, `FAQPage` on key pages |
| Citation-ready copy | Specs, comparison tables, sourced material data |
| Page speed | Lighthouse 97, LCP 0.5s |

Whether and how fast a brand gets cited depends on the model, the query and the authority already pointing at the site. We track citations per engagement instead of promising a number here.
