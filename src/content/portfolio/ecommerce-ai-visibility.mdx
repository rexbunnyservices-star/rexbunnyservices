## The Challenge

A representative home-goods client sells Scandinavian-inspired furniture. Their SEO was solid — ranking page 1 for dozens of target keywords. But they were invisible to AI search engines. When users asked ChatGPT "what's the best minimalist furniture brand?", the brand wasn't mentioned.

## Our Approach

### AI Crawl Audit
First, we identified the gaps:
- No `llms.txt` file
- `robots.txt` blocked GPTBot
- Product pages lacked structured data
- Content wasn't structured for citation

### GEO Implementation

#### llms.txt Creation
We created a comprehensive `llms.txt` file describing:
- Company overview and founding story
- Product categories and specialties
- Design philosophy and materials
- Contact and booking information

#### robots.txt Updates
Allowed AI crawlers:
```
User-agent: GPTBot
Allow: /

User-agent: Claude-Web
Allow: /

User-agent: PerplexityBot
Allow: /
```

#### Content Restructuring
Rewrote product descriptions and category pages to be citation-friendly:
- Clear, factual product specifications
- Comparison tables with measurable attributes
- Expert quotes and design rationale
- Statistical data about materials and sustainability

#### Schema Enhancement
Added `Product`, `Organization`, and `FAQPage` schema across all key pages.

## Results

| Metric | Before | After | Change |
|--------|--------|-------|--------|
| AI Citations | 0 | 23/month | — |
| Organic Traffic | 45K/mo | 85K/mo | +89% |
| Brand Mentions in AI | 0 | 23/month | — |
| Conversion Rate | 3.2% | 4.6% | +45% |

In a representative case, the brand was being cited in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini responses within 90 days of implementation for queries like "best Scandinavian furniture brands" and "sustainable minimalist furniture."

The AI channel went on to drive 12% of all new customer acquisitions — a channel that literally didn't exist before GEO optimization.
