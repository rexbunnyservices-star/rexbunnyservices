## The Challenge

A representative neighborhood coffee shop was competing against chain stores with massive marketing budgets. Their social media relied on smartphone photos — grainy, inconsistent, and low engagement. A professional photoshoot was quoted at $3,500+ which was out of budget.

## Our Approach

### AI-Generated Product Photography
We used our ComfyUI workflows to create professional-grade product photos:
- **Remove Background (BiRefNet)** — Clean cutouts of coffee cups, pastries, and merchandise
- **Image Edit (Flux.2 Dev)** — Placed products into appealing lifestyle scenes
- **Image Upscale (Z-Image-Turbo)** — 4K output for crisp social media posts

### Video Ads for Social
We turned their existing photos into engaging video content:
- **Image to Video (Wan 2.2)** — Animated still photos into 5-second clips
- **Text to Video (LTX-2.3)** — Branded background loops with coffee shop ambiance
- **Audio Generation (ACE-Step 1.5)** — Custom background music matching their brand vibe

### Consistent Brand Aesthetic
Applied uniform color grading across all assets using our color adjustment workflows, creating a cohesive Instagram feed that looked professionally produced.

## Results

| Metric | Before | After | Change |
|--------|--------|-------|--------|
| Instagram Engagement Rate | 1.2% | 4.6% | +280% |
| Weekly Post Reach | 2,100 | 12,400 | +490% |
| Video Views (first month) | 0 | 52,000 | New channel |
| Total Creative Cost | $0 (phone photos) | $997 (one-time) | vs $3,500 photoshoot |

The coffee shop now has a content library of 50+ professional images and 10+ video ads — all generated for under $1,000. They post daily with zero creative bottlenecks.
