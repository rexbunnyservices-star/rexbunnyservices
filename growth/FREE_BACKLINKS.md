# REX Bunny Services — Free Backlink Plan (vetted, no spam)

**Rule:** We only pursue links a real editor/user would place. No PBNs, no $5 Fiverr
packages, no link farms. Our linkable asset is the **free AI Visibility Audit** —
a genuinely useful tool, which is what earns editorial links in 2026.

Baseline: rexbunnyservices.online — 82 sessions/30d. Target: 20-30 quality
referring domains in 90 days via the list below.

---

## TIER 1 — Highest authority, free, do this first

### 1. Product Hunt — launch the free audit tool
- URL: https://www.producthunt.com/ (submit a product; free)
- Authority: ~DA 93. One launch = 1 strong link + real early users.
- What to submit: "AI Visibility Audit — free 18-point scorecard for ChatGPT / Perplexity / Gemini"
- Copy (tagline): *"See if ChatGPT can find your business. 18 live checks, scorecard you can share, PDF in 30 seconds."*
- Copy (first comment): explain the problem (AI search quotes entities it can verify), what the tool checks, and that it is free with no signup wall beyond email for the PDF.
- Effort: M (need images/video). Measure: Product Hunt referral sessions in Umami.

### 2. There's An AI For That (TAAFT) — submit the tool
- URL: https://theresanaiforthat.com/submit/
- Authority: ~DA 80, huge AI-tool directory, genuinely relevant.
- Category: SEO / Marketing. Free submission.
- Copy: same as Product Hunt tagline.
- Effort: S (form). Measure: referral traffic.

### 3. Qwoted / Featured / SourceBottle — journalist sourcing (REAL editorial links)
- URLs: https://www.qwoted.com/ , https://featured.com/ , https://www.sourcebottle.com/
- Free tiers. Journalists post questions; you reply with expert quotes.
- Our angle: we run a lead engine that scored 1,629,917 prospects and audit sites for AI visibility — genuine expertise on "how AI search decides what to quote", "llms.txt", "GEO vs SEO", "local SEO in 2026".
- Action: create free profiles, set alerts for keywords: `SEO`, `ChatGPT`, `AI search`, `generative engine optimization`, `local business marketing`, `website`.
- Reply fast (within hours) with a 2-3 sentence expert answer + link to the matching blog post (e.g. /blog/get-cited-in-chatgpt). These links are on news sites (DA 70-90).
- Effort: M (ongoing, 15 min/day). Measure: reply → published link.

### 4. Podcast guest appearances (very high authority, free)
- Directories: https://podcastguests.com/ , https://matchmaker.fm/ (free tiers)
- Pitch angle: "How to get your business cited in ChatGPT" — founder story: one person, automated pipeline, real before/after numbers.
- Target: marketing, SEO, AI, and small-business podcasts.
- Each appearance usually includes a show-notes link to rexbunnyservices.online.
- Effort: M. Measure: referral + brand searches.
