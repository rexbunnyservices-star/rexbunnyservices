# Backlink Execution Plan — 30 days to traffic

Goal: 20–30 quality referring domains in 90 days, all pointing at the free
AI Visibility Audit (`https://rexbunnyservices.online/rex/`), which converts
each visitor into a shareable scorecard.

Files:
- `FREE_BACKLINKS.md` — where to get links (targets)
- `OUTREACH_COPY.md` — what to say (copy)
- `backlink_tracker.csv` — what is live / pending (status)
- This file — when and how to execute

---

## Ground rules
1. **One tracked link = one UTM.** Every link we place or control uses
   `?utm_source=<source>&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=backlink`.
   Umami records the referrer automatically; the UTM makes attribution exact.
2. **Never send bulk email.** Outreach is 1:1, personalized, from
   `help@rexbunnyservices.com`. Max 10/day. No mail-merge.
3. **Verify every link.** When a site publishes, open the page, confirm the
   link is live and points to the right URL, then update the tracker.
4. **Log the date contacted.** If no reply in 7 days, send ONE polite follow-up,
   then close the target.
5. **Measure weekly**, not daily. Backlinks compound slowly.

## Daily routine (15 min, every morning)
1. Check email for replies.
2. Reply within the hour if it's an editor/journalist.
3. Update tracker (contacted / follow-up / live).
4. Verify any new live link.
5. Log today's actions in `growth/backlink_log.md` (create if missing).

---

## What success looks like
- A journalist link on a DA 70+ site → 1 strong link + immediate traffic spike.
- Product Hunt launch → 1 link + a cohort of early users who share scorecards.
- 20 directory/profile links → entity authority (helps AEO/GEO, our own pitch).
- Guest posts → 2–3 contextual links on authority sites.
- Every visitor → free audit → shareable scorecard → their network → more links.

The backlink is the seed. The scorecard is the propagation engine.
